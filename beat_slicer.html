BEAT SLICER

Orbs launch evenly on the BPM grid at a constant speed. Active sections add half-beats. Slice after the ring appears: cutting too early costs 150 points, a missed orb — 100. Stop the mouse and the game waits with no penalties.

Track file — the most accurate sync (beats are analyzed ahead of time). Tab audio — Chrome only: play music in another tab, pick it and tick “Also share tab audio”.
